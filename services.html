Our Core Services

From hands-on threat monitoring and IT compliance audits to enterprise data pipelines and executive dashboards — our services turn complex technical risk into operational clarity.

Practice 01 • Cybersecurity

Security Analytics & Threat Monitoring

Engage Threat Practice

Traditional security setups bury engineers in thousands of false-positive alerts. DreaMarz deploys data-driven security analytics to continuously ingest, correlate, and prioritize threat telemetry across your cloud accounts, identity providers, and endpoints.

SIEM & Log Telemetry Optimization

Architecture and fine-tuning for Splunk, Elastic, Microsoft Sentinel, and Datadog to capture critical audit trails without exorbitant ingestion bills.

Behavioral Anomaly Detection

Statistical and heuristic modeling to identify anomalous lateral movement, credential abuse, and unusual data exfiltration in real-time.

Alert Triage & Noise Reduction

Custom detection rule authoring and alert suppression runbooks that reduce alert fatigue by up to 75% for internal security and ops teams.

Key Deliverables: Centralized SIEM Architecture, Custom Detection Catalog, 24/7 Escalation Playbook. Active Ingestion Tested
Practice 02 • Cybersecurity & Governance

IT Audit, Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

Prepare for Audit

Navigating regulatory frameworks like SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA doesn't have to stall your engineering velocity. We replace manual screenshot-grabbing with automated evidence collection and pragmatic security controls.

SOC 2 Type I & Type II Readiness

Comprehensive gap assessments, technical remediation roadmaps, auditor coordination, and continuous control monitoring.

ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS Implementation

Design and documentation of your Information Security Management System (ISMS), risk register, and Annex A control implementations.

Vendor Risk & Third-Party Governance

Standardized third-party security evaluations, SIG Lite / CAIQ questionnaires, and automated vendor risk tiering workflows.

Key Deliverables: Gap Matrix, Security Policies Suite, Automated Evidence Repository, Auditor-Facing Package. Zero Deficiency Target
Practice 03 • Cybersecurity & Incident Response

Security Investigations & Forensics

Request Emergency Response

When an anomaly occurs or an incident escalates, speed and precision are paramount. DreaMarz conducts rigorous digital forensics, root-cause investigations, and data exposure assessments to contain threats and preserve evidence chains.

Rapid Incident Triage & Containment

Immediate threat isolation, network segment fencing, revoked token rotations, and compromised asset containment.

Root-Cause Forensics & Blast Radius

Reconstruction of attacker timelines from disk images, memory dumps, cloud trail telemetry, and access logs to determine exact blast radius.

Executive & Regulatory Post-Mortems

Legally defensible forensic reports for board directors, insurers, legal counsel, and regulatory notification requirements.

Key Deliverables: Timeline Reconstruction, Forensic Artifacts Dossier, Remediated Hardening Plan. Chain of Custody Assured
Practice 04 • Data Intelligence

Data Analytics, Dashboards & Visualization

Build Your Dashboard

Data is useless if leaders cannot interpret it. We build executive-grade visual intelligence portals and BI models that unify disparate databases, sales telemetry, security metrics, and operations into one responsive pane of glass.

C-Suite KPI Portals

Tailored dashboards in Tableau, Power BI, Looker, or custom React/Web apps designed specifically for executive and board decision-makers.

Security & Operational Telemetry Views

Real-time monitoring visualizations showing vulnerability burndown, SLA compliance, customer transaction velocity, and uptime metrics.

Predictive & Heuristic Analytics

Trend extrapolation and automated threshold anomaly alerts delivered straight into Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Key Deliverables: Interactive BI Models, Role-Based Access Views, Automated Scheduled Digests. Sub-Second Refresh Optimized
Practice 05 • Data Engineering

Data Services: Cleaning, Pipelines & Reporting

Optimize Data Pipelines

Dashboards and security controls are only as reliable as the underlying pipelines. DreaMarz architects high-throughput, fault-tolerant ETL/ELT data pipelines with automated data hygiene, validation checks, and data warehouse integration.

Modern Data Stack Architecture

Deployment and orchestration across Snowflake, BigQuery, AWS Redshift, Databricks, dbt, and Airflow.

Automated Data Cleansing & Deduplication

Eliminate corrupted records, handle schema drift gracefully, and maintain data purity with automated assertion tests.

Secure Telemetry Ingestion

End-to-end encryption in flight and at rest, automated PII tokenization/masking, and compliance-grade audit logging.

Key Deliverables: Production Pipeline Codebase, Data Dictionary, Cleansing Scripts, Automated Test Suites. 99.99% Reliability SLA
